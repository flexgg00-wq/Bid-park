Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do real-time bids work?
Bids update instantaneously using Socket.io WebSockets. Every new high bid is broadcast live to all active buyers on the marketplace without needing to refresh the page.
2. How do I list an item as a seller?
Access the Seller Portal, log in or sign up, specify the item title and starting price, upload an image file directly from your device, and post it immediately to the marketplace.
3. What happens when an auction closes?
When an administrator closes an auction, the winning bidder receives an automated notification email containing payment verification details and checkout links.
4. Do I need an account to view items?
No, anyone can explore and view active auction listings on the marketplace. An account is only required when you choose to place a bid or post an item as a seller.
